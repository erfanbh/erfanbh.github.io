About Me

Hello, I’m Erfan, a passionate about the fascinating intersection of Acoustics and Machine Learning. Currently, I'm a PhD student at the University of Washington, where I'm delving into the world of Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) Technology in the oceans.
With expertise in programming languages ​​such as Python and Matlab, I focus on leveraging DAS technology to benefit oceanic environments and unravel the mysteries of the ocean's depths.
In this dynamic field, the data is as vast as the ocean itself, and I thrive on tackling the challenges of working with big data, data processing, and advanced data analysis techniques. My goal is to unlock valuable insights from this sea of ​​information, enabling innovative solutions for ocean-related applications.
